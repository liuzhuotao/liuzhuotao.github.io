Hi, this is Jinzhu Yan (闫金柱). I am currently a Ph.D. student with a research background in machine learning and networked systems. During my Ph.D. studies, my research has focused on AI-driven Network Traffic Analysis systems, aiming to optimize these systems across three key dimensions: generalizable accuracy, adversarial robustness and deployment efficiency. My most recent research centers on Agentic Reasoning for Traffic Analysis, where I focus on empowering Large Language Models with domain-specific expert knowledge and operational toolchains to build an expert-level traffic analysis agent.
