## Open Opportunities

We have multiple openings for **Ph.D. students, master's students, undergraduate students, and postdoctoral researchers**. Our group is well funded, and we welcome people who enjoy exploring new ideas and working together.

## Postdoctoral Positions

In addition to the benefits provided by Tsinghua University, our group offers **RMB 200,000–300,000 in additional annual compensation** to outstanding postdoctoral applicants. Please get in touch to discuss opportunities.

## How We Work

Research can take years of commitment. Building a good working culture matters deeply to me: I want every member to grow both as a researcher and as a person.

- **Friendly.** We treat one another with mutual respect. Everyone, including me, is part of the same team.
- **Supportive.** We help one another through the challenges and successes of research.
- **Flourishing.** We support each member's academic growth and preparation for what comes next.

## Start a Conversation

If our research interests overlap, feel free to email me. Tell me which opportunity interests you and a little about your background. You are welcome to include a link to work you would like to share; a brief introduction is a good place to start.
